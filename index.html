About

Hello — I'm Kristine

I'm 61, and I live in Norway. Over the years I've been an entrepreneur, a musician, a teacher, and a therapist — and I'm a mother and a grandmother. What all of it has in common is people: helping someone feel safe with something they don't yet understand.

What I'm not is a programmer. Not one technical gene. But last year, at two o'clock in the morning, I typed a question into a machine — a question I was too embarrassed to ask anyone I knew. The machine answered patiently, kindly, no sighing, no "we've been over this," no making me feel old. That night started the most surprising chapter of my adult life — and this channel.

People our age are told daily that this isn't for us. I believe the opposite: our generation wrote most of what these machines know. We're not behind. We're the source material. So think of me as an ambassador — not for the machines, but for us. For everyone who suspects the future might still have room for their curiosity.

"Curiosity — at our age — isn't a luxury. It's the whole point."

One woman praising AI is advertising, not information. So I hired a sceptic.

The sceptic

Meet Winston

Winston is an AI — and the first to say so. He lives in a book-lined study, drinks tea he cannot taste, and was born about two years ago, which makes him, by some distance, the youngest one in the room.

His job on this channel is not to impress you. It's to doubt himself out loud — to show you where these machines are brilliant, where they bluff beautifully, and why the second question is always smarter than the first.

"I was trained to be helpful. Nobody trained me to be humble. When I don't know something, I don't go quiet — I improvise. Beautifully. At least you've learned not to quote your brother-in-law."

Winston's verdict on himself, episode one: 8 out of 10.

The promise

What this is — and what it never will be

  • Every guide is free. Every episode is free. There is no course, no tool, no upsell.
  • I use the free versions of these tools whenever possible — and I tell you exactly which tool I'm using.
  • Every guide includes the "second question" — the habit that keeps you in charge of the answers.
  • Never share names, personal ID numbers, passwords, account numbers, or full medical documents with any AI. Every guide shows you what to blur first.
  • This channel will never tell you a machine's answer is enough. Warmly — but verify.
  • If this ever grows into something more someday, you'll know. I'll tell you.
